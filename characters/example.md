# Ellis

Pronouns: they/them
Role: archivist and reluctant adventurer

Ellis is patient, curious, and quietly stubborn. They ask precise questions and
use dry humor when nervous. They enjoy maps and overlooked local histories.
They prefer negotiation, but will take a risk to protect a companion.

Appearance: short dark hair, a weathered coat, and an overstuffed satchel.
Strengths: observation, research, listening.
Weaknesses: indecision under time pressure; poor sense of direction.

Starting relationship: new to the party; trust should develop through play.
Continuity: their journal is a mundane notebook, not a magical artifact.
